Remembered on this device. Shown next to any on-demand backup you take in the Audit Trail and Telegram alert.

A snapshot of every registration, invitation, check-in and audit-log row is taken automatically every night at 01:00 Cairo time, plus whenever you click "Run backup now" — e.g. right before a bulk edit or merge. The 30 most recent snapshots are kept; older ones are pruned automatically.

Attachments

Passport copies, flight itineraries, speaker photos/slides, and accompanying persons' passport copies aren't included in the snapshots above — they already live in R2, separately from the database. This downloads every one of them to your computer as a single organised ZIP, one folder per person.
